Do You Qualify?

  • ✓ Age 25-55 years old
  • ✓ Experiencing androgenetic alopecia (male pattern hair loss)
  • ✓ Norwood scale 2-5 (mild to moderate hair loss)
  • ✓ Willing to attend 6 study visits over 12 months
  • ✓ No current hair loss treatments (2 week washout period OK)
  • ✓ Generally healthy with no autoimmune conditions

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the R-Spondin trial

Is the treatment really free?

Yes. All R-Spondin treatments, medical monitoring, and study-related procedures are provided at no cost to participants.

What are the potential side effects?

In preclinical studies, R-Spondin has shown a favorable safety profile. All potential risks will be discussed during screening.

How much time does it require?

Approximately 6 visits over 12 months, each lasting about 1-2 hours. You'll be compensated $150 per visit.

Can I continue other treatments?

You'll need to stop other hair loss treatments before the trial. A 2-week washout period is typically sufficient.

Where is the trial located?

Trial site locations are To Be Announced. Enter your email to be notified when sites are confirmed in your area.

What happens after the trial?

Participants may have the option to continue treatment through an extension study based on results and FDA guidance.
