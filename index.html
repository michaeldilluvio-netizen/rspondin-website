The Science of R-Spondin

Understanding how R-Spondin proteins amplify Wnt signaling to promote hair follicle regeneration and growth

🧬

R-Spondin Proteins

R-Spondins (RSPO1-4) are secreted proteins that potentiate Wnt signaling by binding to LGR receptors. They prevent the degradation of Wnt receptors, amplifying the signaling cascade essential for hair follicle stem cell activation.

🔄

Wnt/β-Catenin Pathway

The canonical Wnt pathway is critical for hair follicle development and cycling. When activated, β-catenin accumulates and translocates to the nucleus, triggering genes responsible for hair growth and regeneration.

🌱

Hair Follicle Stem Cells

Hair follicle stem cells reside in the bulge region and are activated during the anagen (growth) phase. R-Spondin enhances Wnt signals that awaken these dormant stem cells, promoting new hair cycle initiation.

Future Therapeutics

Our roadmap for developing R-Spondin-based treatments for hair loss

Phase 1

Target Validation

Confirming R-Spondin/Wnt pathway components as viable therapeutic targets through comprehensive preclinical studies and molecular analysis.

Phase 2

Drug Discovery

Developing small molecules and biologics that can safely modulate R-Spondin signaling in hair follicle stem cells.

Phase 3

Preclinical Development

Rigorous testing of lead compounds in validated hair loss models, optimizing delivery methods and safety profiles.

Phase 4

Clinical Trials

Human clinical trials to evaluate safety, efficacy, and optimal dosing of R-Spondin pathway modulators for hair regeneration.
