Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2023

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to HairDAO Association's websites, applications, and services, including this R-Spondin clinical trial enrollment site and related mobile applications.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Individual-level Information: Information about a single individual, such as hair loss data and eligibility responses
  • Registration Details: Name, email address, phone number, and date of birth
  • Self-reported Health Information: Hair loss stage, treatment history, and related health data
  • Web Behavior Data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and usage patterns

How We Collect Information

  • Directly from you during registration and eligibility screening
  • Through third-party service providers using cookies and analytics
  • From other users and corporate affiliates
  • Through automated systems that infer data from your interactions

How We Use Your Information

HairDAO uses your information to:

  • Assess your eligibility for the R-Spondin clinical trial
  • Communicate with you about the trial and related services
  • Provide and improve our services
  • Analyze usage trends and optimize user experience
  • Conduct research (with your consent) using de-identified data
  • Enhance security and prevent fraud

Research Use

HairDAO may conduct independent research using your personal information for making new discoveries about hair loss. Research uses de-identified data only, meaning your identity will not be disclosed in any research publications or findings.

Data Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Third parties who help us operate our services, bound by contractual protections
  • Clinical Trial Partners: Medical professionals and research institutions involved in the R-Spondin trial
  • Corporate Affiliates: During mergers, acquisitions, or corporate restructuring
  • Law Enforcement: Only with valid court orders or legal requirements

Security

HairDAO implements physical, technical, and administrative protections to safeguard your information. You are responsible for protecting your passwords and must notify HairDAO immediately of any unauthorized access to your account.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your information
  • Opt out of certain data uses
  • Data portability

U.S. residents (CA, VA, CO) and EEA, UK, Switzerland residents have additional protections under local laws.

Data Retention

We retain your data as long as necessary for service delivery, legal compliance, and legitimate business purposes. Clinical trial data may be retained longer as required by regulatory guidelines.

Third-Party Services

HairDAO is not responsible for third-party privacy practices. Please review their policies separately when using external services linked from our site.
